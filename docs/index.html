Features

Find it, track it, plan it.

One app for the whole evening, from deciding what to watch to remembering where you left off.

  • Tonight

    A home feed of trending titles, new streaming arrivals and picks from the services you pay for. Open the app and something good is already waiting.

  • Where to Watch

    See which services stream, rent or sell a title in your region. One tap takes you straight into the service.

  • Watchlist

    Want to Watch, Watching, Watched, Dropped. Track episode-level progress and rate anything out of five stars.

  • Planner

    Schedule viewing nights, add them to your calendar and get reminders. Upcoming episodes of the shows you follow appear right alongside.

  • Shuffle

    Can't decide? Filter by mood and the time you have, then let Shuffle pick. One more tap if you'd rather see what's behind door number two.

  • Moods

    Browse by how you feel instead of by genre. Each mood is its own lane of titles that fit it.

    • Cozy
    • Laughs
    • Thrills
    • Feels
    • Spooky
    • Brainy

  • Sync

    Apple devices sync through your own iCloud, with no account to create. Android imports and exports the same JSON, so your library moves with you.

Platforms

At home on every screen.

Native on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with a Jetpack Compose app for Android phones, tablets and foldables.

Build

Build it yourself.

Couch Potato is open source. Clone the repository and run it on your own devices in a few minutes.

Apple

iPhone, iPad and Mac

  • Xcode26 or later
  • iOS / iPadOS26 or later
  • macOS26 or later
  • XcodeGenvia Homebrew
brew install xcodegengit clone https://github.com/nearbycoder/couch-potatocd couch-potatoxcodegenopen CouchPotato.xcodeproj

Android

Phones, tablets and foldables

  • Android StudioLadybug or later
  • JDK17 or later
cd couch-potato/android./gradlew :app:installDebug

Optional: add a free TMDB API key in Settings → Data sources to unlock every title and all-provider availability. Without one the app uses TVmaze and Apple's iTunes charts.
