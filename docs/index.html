Tonight

Open it. Know what's on.

A home screen built around tonight: big featured artwork, what's trending this week, new episodes on your services, and the next thing from your watchlist, all a tap away.

  • Up Next picks up where your watchlist left off.
  • New on Streaming shows episodes that just dropped.
  • Popular on your services, row by row.

Sync

One library on every device.

Add a show on your iPhone and it's on your iPad and Mac a moment later. Your watchlist, ratings, progress and plans sync through iCloud, with no account to create.

Up to date

iCloud, automatically

Every iPhone, iPad and Mac on your Apple ID shares one library. Settings shows when it last synced.

Android, too

Export your library and import it on the other side. It merges, and the newest changes win.

Private by design

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your library lives on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Build

Run it yourself.

Couch Potato is open source. Clone the repository and have it on your own devices in a few minutes.

Apple

iPhone, iPad and Mac

  • Xcode26 or later
  • iOS / iPadOS26 or later
  • macOS26 or later
  • XcodeGenvia Homebrew
brew install xcodegengit clone https://github.com/nearbycoder/couch-potatocd couch-potatoxcodegenopen CouchPotato.xcodeproj

Android

Phones, tablets and foldables

  • Android StudioLadybug or later
  • JDK17 or later
cd couch-potato/android./gradlew :app:installDebug

Optional: add a free TMDB API key in Settings → Data Sources to include movies and streaming availability in every region. Without one, the app uses TVmaze, Apple's movie charts and Couch Potato's own series catalog.
