Privacy Policy

The short version: Couch Potato has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no analytics or tracking. Your watchlist and plans live on your devices and in your own iCloud. We never see them. Spud is nosy about TV, not about you.

Your stuff stays yours

  • There's no sign-up and no Couch Potato account.
  • Your watchlist, statuses, episode progress, ratings, couch rank and planned couch nights are stored on your device.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, that data syncs between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. It's governed by Apple's privacy policy, and the developer can't read it.

Where the TV info comes from

To show titles, artwork, episode schedules and where things are streaming, the app asks these public services for catalog data:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database), only if you choose to add your own free API key in Settings → Data Sources. That key is stored on your device.
  • TVmaze, for TV show details and episode schedules.
  • Apple's public iTunes feeds, for charts and artwork.

These requests contain things like search terms, title IDs and your region, so the right results come back. They don't include your watchlist or anything that identifies you. Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your device connects to them, and their own privacy policies apply.

Optional permissions

  • Calendar: only if you tap to add a couch night to Apple Calendar. The app adds the event you asked for and nothing else.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. They're local notifications scheduled on your device, not pushed from a server.

You can change either one any time in your device's Settings.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, ad SDKs, trackers or fingerprinting.
  • No selling, sharing or collecting of personal data. There's nothing to sell.
  • No hidden servers. Couch Potato is open source, so you can read the code and check.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove local data. To remove synced data, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage on your device and delete Couch Potato's data.

Questions or changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. TV data provided by TVmaze, licensed under CC BY-SA.
