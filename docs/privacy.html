Privacy

Privacy Policy

Couch Potato has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your watchlist, ratings and plans stay on your device and, on Apple platforms, in your own iCloud. There is nothing for us to collect, so we don't.

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no servers. The app never signs you in and never talks to a server we run.
  • Your data stays yours. Apple devices sync through your own iCloud; Android keeps everything on the device.
  • No analytics or tracking. No ads, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs.
  • Catalog lookups go directly to TMDB, TVmaze and Apple. Those services see the requests, not us.

What the app stores

Couch Potato keeps only what it needs to work: the titles on your watchlist and their status (Want to Watch, Watching, Watched, Dropped), episode progress, your star ratings, planned viewing nights, your mood and time preferences, the streaming services you say you subscribe to, and app settings. If you choose to add a TMDB API key, that key is stored alongside your settings.

Where it is stored

iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your data lives on the device and, when iCloud is enabled, in your own iCloud account using CloudKit's private database and the iCloud key-value store. Only you and your signed-in devices can read it. Apple hosts that data under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it and no way to see it.

Android. Your data is stored on the device only. The app can export it to a JSON file and import that file again. You decide where that file goes and who can see it; the app never uploads it anywhere.

Catalog data and artwork

To show titles, artwork and streaming availability, the app fetches data from third-party services. These requests go directly from your device to the service; nothing passes through us.

  • TMDB (The Movie Database), only if you add your own API key. Requests to TMDB include that key and the titles you search for or open. Streaming availability shown by the app comes from JustWatch data provided through TMDB. See the TMDB privacy policy.
  • TVmaze, for series and episode data. See the TVmaze privacy policy.
  • Apple's public iTunes endpoints, for charts and movie metadata when no TMDB key is set. See Apple's privacy policy.

Like any web request, these services can see your device's IP address and the titles you look up. The app sends nothing else: no identifiers, no contacts, no location.

Optional permissions

  • Calendar. If you choose to add a viewing night to your calendar, the app asks for calendar access and uses it only to create and update those events. It does not read or scan your other events.
  • Notifications. If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves the device.

Both permissions are optional. The app works without them, and you can revoke them at any time in your system settings.

Analytics and tracking

None. Couch Potato contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not fingerprint your device. We cannot see how you use the app, and we would not want to.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. On Apple platforms, data already synced to iCloud can be removed from Settings → Your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Couch Potato on iPhone and iPad, or System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage on a Mac. On Android, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes its data; delete any JSON files you exported yourself.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Because the app has no servers, a change cannot affect data the app has already stored on your devices or in your iCloud.

Questions

Couch Potato is open source, so you can read exactly what the app does in its source code. For questions about privacy, open an issue on GitHub.
